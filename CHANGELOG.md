# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — preview

- Menu-bar control for AdGuard DNS or a custom filtering resolver.
- Per-service backups with exact DNS restoration and conflict checks.
- Recovery after interrupted operations and failed enables.
- Installer, emergency restore script, and local diagnostics.
- Automated tests for restoration, rollback, input validation, and journal safety.

This preview targets macOS 14 and later. The downloadable build is for Apple Silicon and is locally signed, without Apple notarization.
